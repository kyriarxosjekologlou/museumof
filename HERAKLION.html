Heraklion Archaeological Museum

heraklion archaeological museumThe Heraklion Archaeological Museum is one of the most prominent museums in Europe and houses the world's most important collection of Minoan antiquities. Located in the city center of Heraklion, Crete, it spans over 5,500 years of history, from the Neolithic era to the Roman period. It serves as an essential companion visit to the Knossos Palace archaeological site
